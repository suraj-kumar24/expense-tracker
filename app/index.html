Budgeting that fits in a chai break

⚡

Log it before you forget

Type the amount or slide to it, tap a category, done. Save quick amounts like your daily chai, and fixed bills like rent get filed for you on the 1st.

🚦

Budgets that nudge

Every category shows how much is left. It turns amber when you’re getting close and red when you’re almost out. You choose when.

🎬

A daily allowance for fun

Mark eating out, shopping or movies as Wants. Khata divides what’s left by the days left, so you know what today can take.

📈

See the whole year

Month by month or across twelve months: your biggest slices, your highest and lowest months, and what changed since last month.

Your money stays on your phone

No account, no login, no servers. Everything you log is saved on your device and works without internet. Want a copy? Export a spreadsheet any time from Settings.

Get Khata on your phone

It installs straight from the browser in a few seconds, and opens from your home screen like any other app.

Android

  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap ⋮, then Install app (or Add to Home screen).
  3. Open Khata from your home screen.

iPhone

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button ⬆︎.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.

On a computer?

Scan with your phone’s camera to open this page there.
